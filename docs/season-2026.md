## 2026 season settings

### Regular season rounds

For 2026 we treat the NRL Telstra Premiership **regular season** as **27 rounds**.

Source:
- NRL draw: `https://www.nrl.com/draw/`

This value is checked into `data/season_meta.json` as `totalRegularRounds: 27` and used to render the round selector even when future rounds haven't been baked yet.
